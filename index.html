Frequently Addressed Inquiries

What is the verified lifespan of a commercial vehicle wrap?

Our 3M and Avery Dennison cast films provide an operational lifespan of 5 to 7 years when maintained with standard automotive cleaning practices. Every full wrap includes a 5 year surface warranty against premature fading, peeling, and lifting.

Will the vinyl removal process damage our factory paint?

No. High grade cast vinyl utilizes clean release adhesives engineered specifically for OEM clearcoats. When removed by our technicians using controlled infrared heat, the factory paint underneath remains in original condition.

What is the required turnaround time per commercial vehicle?

A standard cargo van or pickup truck requires 2 to 3 business days from vehicle drop off to final delivery. For multi vehicle commercial fleets, we coordinate phased rolling schedules to keep your operations active.

Do you offer volume pricing for multi vehicle commercial fleets?

Yes. Fleet deployments of 3 or more vehicles qualify for itemized volume tiering, synchronized print runs, and dedicated bay scheduling to minimize fleet idle time.
