Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding commercial vehicle wrapping and maintenance.

How long does a commercial vehicle wrap last?

With proper care and maintenance, our premium 3M and Avery Dennison cast vinyl wraps last between 5 to 7 years in typical Texas climate conditions.

Will the wrap damage my vehicle's factory paint?

No. High-grade cast vinyl preserves OEM factory paint from UV fading and road abrasion. Original paint remains intact upon professional removal.

What is the turnaround time per vehicle?

Following design approval, printing and installation typically require 2 to 4 business days per vehicle to allow proper vinyl post-heating and cure.

Do you provide partial wraps and fleet decals?

Yes. We engineer solutions across every tier: full vehicle wraps, partial rear/side wraps, perforated window graphics, and cut vinyl lettering.
