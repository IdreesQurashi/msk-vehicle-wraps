Frequently Addressed Inquiries

What is the lifespan of commercial cast vinyl in Texas climate?
When engineered with premium 3M or Avery Dennison cast vinyl and UV-resistant over-laminate, our commercial wraps maintain a rated service life of 5 to 7 years under standard Texas environmental conditions.
Does professional wrap installation affect factory OEM paint?
No. High-grade cast film actively protects the underlying factory clear-coat from UV degradation, road gravel, and abrasion. The original paint remains pristine upon professional removal.
What is the turnaround schedule per commercial vehicle?
Following digital proof approval, printing, laminating, and installation typically require 2 to 4 business days per vehicle to allow proper post-heating and adhesive cure.
Can you accommodate partial wraps and enterprise fleet decal kits?
Yes. We deliver solutions spanning full vehicle coverage, partial side/rear branding, perforated architectural window film, and precision cut vinyl lettering.
