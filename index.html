Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about vehicle wrapping in Houston.

How long does a commercial vehicle wrap last?

With proper care and maintenance, our premium 3M and Avery Dennison cast vinyl wraps last between 5 to 7 years in typical Texas climate conditions.

Will the wrap damage my vehicle's factory paint?

No! High-grade vinyl actually protects OEM factory paint from UV fading and scratches. When professionally removed, your original paint remains intact and preserved.

What is the turnaround time for a wrap?

Once design proofs are approved, printing and installation typically take 2 to 4 business days per vehicle to ensure proper post-heating and vinyl adhesion.

Do you offer partial wraps and decals?

Yes. We customize every project to your budget, from full vehicle coverage to partial rear/side wraps, perforated window film, and spot decals.
